The placement litmus

By the end you can take any item on your desk and put it in the right ledger with one question — instead of discovering three weeks later that a decision was queued as a delivery and never closed.

Two ledgers, never merged

The fleet keeps work in two places that look alike and close differently. A roadmap slice is a delivery: it closes when a pull request merges. A wayfinder ticket is a question: it closes when someone answers it. Mixing them breaks both closure semantics — answers never touch status.jsonl. SKILL.md, Gotchas

There is a third place, and it is the one people skip: fog. Fog is the section of a map for things you cannot yet phrase as a question. It is not a backlog of laziness — it is an honest record that the question itself has not formed. ADR-0101 §Placement

The litmus

One question sorts all three. Ask it about the item in front of you:

What you can stateWhere it goesHow it closes
success and a machine-runnable check Roadmap slice A merged PR
Only the question, precisely Wayfinder ticket An answer, recorded
Not even the question yet Map fog It graduates to a ticket

Note what the litmus does not ask: how big the work is, how long it takes, or how confident you feel. Size routes nothing. A one-line config change whose correct value is genuinely undecided is a ticket; a two-week migration with a clear check is a slice. SKILL.md, Core principles §3

The direction of travel

Items move one way: fog → ticket → slice. Fog graduates when the question becomes statable — not when the answer does. A ticket becomes slices only at handoff, once enough decisions are closed that entrance, success and check can all be written down. That is why a map is called a slice refinery. SKILL.md, Handoff

The failure this prevents is specific and common: a slice sits queued for months because nobody can assert its entrance criterion. That is not a stalled delivery. That is fog wearing a delivery's clothes. SKILL.md, Gotchas

Check yourself

1. “Add a --strict flag to bead-lint that fails CI on non-conformant hooks.”

2. What makes a wayfinder ticket closeable?

3. A question you cannot yet phrase precisely belongs where?
