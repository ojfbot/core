# MISSION

Interrogated 2026-08-04. Topic as given was a *subject* ("matt pocock skills library and philosophy
and approach to building harnesses and software fundamentals"); this is the capability it was
interrogated into.

## The capability

**Read an agent's absorb/reject verdict — or a skill an agent wrote — and tell whether the opinion
was taken because it solved a measured local problem, or because it looked right.** Then say the
underlying principle out loud, to someone else, convincingly.

## What the operator said, in their words

- Capability wanted: "2 and 3, 1, to the extent that i can better evaluate agent run output
  **(not looking to increase manual)**"
- Prior contact: "Only through the fleet's output"
- Forcing function: "FDE positioning / articulating the method"
- Suspected wrong model: "I can't tell absorb from cargo-cult"

## What this rules in and out

**In.** Skill-authoring standard (capability 2) and harness-design philosophy (capability 3), taught
*through* the judgment call rather than as doctrine. Stack-judgment (capability 1) enters only as a
reviewing skill.

**Out — and this is the sharp constraint.** The operator is explicitly not trying to do more of this
work by hand. So the target is **review capacity, not production capacity**: the win is a test that
runs in seconds against output an agent already produced. Any lesson that ends in "and now you do
the adopt-stack pass yourself" has missed the mission.

**Out.** His TypeScript/software-fundamentals pedagogy. Named in the original topic string, dropped
here — it is the one branch with no fleet artifact behind it and no connection to the stated
forcing function. Recorded in `RESOURCES.md` `## Gaps` rather than silently discarded.

## Why the suspected gap sets the placement

"I can't tell absorb from cargo-cult" is not a knowledge gap, it is a **discrimination** gap — the
learner has the conclusions (36 of them, in their own repo) and cannot grade them. That is exactly a
zone-of-proximal-development target: they can already read the verdicts, they cannot yet judge them,
and the distance between those is one lesson.

It also converges with the forcing function. Articulating a method to an interviewer fails in
precisely the same place: you can recite what you did, and you cannot say why *that* and not the
shape next to it.

## The test of success

Hand them a verdict they have not seen, from any stack. They can say — cold, without rereading the
source — which of the three discriminator questions it passes, which it fails, and what would have
to be true for the failing one to be defensible.
