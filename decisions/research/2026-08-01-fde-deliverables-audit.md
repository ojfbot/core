# Research — FDE deliverables checklist + market trajectory ("skate to where the puck is going")

**Date:** 2026-08-01 · **Ticket:** wayfinder #320 (map `fde-operating-presence`) · **Method:** deep-research harness, ONE cycle (5 angles → 86 raw claims → top 27 verified, 3 lenses each [source-primacy / recency / generalization]: 25 confirmed, 2 refuted, 0 unverified; 90 agents; run `wf_c49acb71-86d`) · **Feeds:** #327 (hired-projects disposition), #328 (positioning narrative + venue map), #321 (TeamBot boundary) · **Supersedes as audit ruler:** the Bashiri 3-archetype checklist in `2026-07-22-hired-projects-gap-analysis.md` (verified there as repackaged consensus, no primary hiring-side evidence).

## Verdict (one paragraph)

The 2026 FDE market pays for the **consultative arc delivered through harness and eval artifacts** — discovery → prototype on customer data → deployment into the customer's environment → value proven in the customer's numbers, where the named artifacts are MCP servers, sub-agents, agent skills, scaffolding/connector layers, and above all **customer-tailored evaluation frameworks** (a hiring gate at Anthropic, the success metric at OpenAI, the first responsibility at Cohere, the entire deliverable at Braintrust, and the documented interview differentiator: *"How do you know your AI system is actually working?"*). Of the three hypotheses this cycle tested: **H1 (cost-aware orchestration as a rising core deliverable) is REFUTED** — not one verified posting or practitioner source names model routing, pre-run cost estimation, or quota governance as an FDE deliverable; the single genuine hit is one "latency/cost tradeoffs" clause in Cursor's posting, and the strongest cost-tagged evidence actively contradicts the premise (the dominant deployment cost is non-AI enterprise plumbing, not the model). **H2 (eval/gating harnesses) is MIXED** — the eval-framework genre is the single best-evidenced deliverable in the corpus, but the specific sub-claims (HITL gates for AI-generated code, Socratic review capture, senior-reasoning-as-training-data) appear in the harness-engineering discourse owned by dev-tool vendors, not in any FDE evidence, and the verified expertise-capture loop runs **vendor-ward** (FDE patterns codified back to the vendor's product org), not customer-ward. **H3 (the customer-facing core) is SUPPORTED** across all five segments — the market is expanding embedded-human capacity (Accenture: thousands; Salesforce: tripled toward ~1,000; ServiceNow joint program), and harness artifacts are the *medium* of the consultative loop, not a substitute for it.

## Hypothesis verdicts

### H1 — cost-aware orchestration as a rising core FDE deliverable: **REFUTED**

Zero of 25 verified items name model routing by task complexity, pre-run job-cost estimation, spend-vs-value measurement, or quota governance as an FDE deliverable. Four of the five H1-tagged claims turned out to concern *labor* cost, integration effort, or billing — not model cost (tag inflation the judges caught). The one genuine hit is a single "latency/cost tradeoffs" clause among six items in Cursor's production-quality list — a hygiene sub-skill. The strongest cost-adjacent quote points the other way: the dominant deployment cost is **non-AI enterprise plumbing**, per OpenAI's head of DX. A judge spot-check found exactly one posting where cost-aware routing IS the core job — **OpenRouter's FDE** — fully confounded (routing is OpenRouter's product). A claim that "complexity-based model routing is the default 2026 enterprise playbook" was **refuted 0–3** (content-marketing source presented as primary). Verdict: in the 2026 FDE market, cost orchestration is peripheral literacy, not a deliverable. *(This does NOT rule on its fleet/internal value — quota-constrained enterprises still need it; it rules on its weight as FDE positioning evidence.)*

### H2 — eval/gating harnesses as an FDE deliverable genre: **MIXED**

The core is the best-evidenced genre in the corpus (checklist row 1 — every segment, hiring gate, interview differentiator, rising discourse). But the three specific sub-claims tested:
- **HITL quality gates for AI-generated code** — real, rising 2026 discourse ("harness engineering": CodeRabbit, Codacy, TrueFoundry, The New Stack), but owned by engineering leaders and dev-tool vendors; zero FDE evidence. An adjacent-market **bridge play**, not an established FDE genre.
- **Socratic review capture / senior-reasoning as org-specific training data** — appears **nowhere** in the verified corpus. Worse for the framing: the verified expertise-capture loop runs the **opposite direction** — FDEs codify their own deployment patterns back to the *vendor*; no evidence of capturing the *customer's* senior engineers' reasoning as customer-owned training assets. If pursued, it is a novel proposal requiring demand validation, not a market fact to cite.
- **Engineer buy-in / change management** — evidenced as the dominant *cost center* and a delivery *competency*, never a named artifact.

### H3 — the customer-facing consultative core remains what the market pays for: **SUPPORTED**

Every role definition across all five segments centers the full arc; harness artifacts are explicitly built *with/inside* the customer — the medium of the loop, not a substitute. Accenture's founding diagnosis ("not for lack of technology, but for lack of the right engineering expertise applied in the right place") states non-substitution directly; demand-side scaling shows the market expanding embedded-human capacity. Two caveats: "pays for" rests on one comp datapoint plus hiring volume; and the consultative core now has a **mandatory medium** — a candidate with the consultative arc but no harness/eval fluency also fails the 2026 bar.

## Coverage gaps (honest limits of this ONE cycle)

- **No current-generation practitioner first-person accounts** — the practitioner canon reduces to one ~2020 Palantir essay (Nabeel Qureshi); no AI-era FDE at Anthropic/OpenAI/Cursor describing the job from inside made it through verification.
- **100% supply-side corpus** — no CIO/buyer testimony, no analyst reports, no skeptical counter-narrative ("FDE = rebranded sales engineering", margin/burnout/attrition critiques). The corpus is structurally pro-role.
- **One-source claims to handle with care:** Salesforce ~1,000/tripled; "plumbing dominates cost" (one org, twice, via secondary summaries); Devin 3/20 + pass@k/pass^k framing (AIE report citing Answer.ai; primary not pulled); eval-question-as-differentiator (commercial interview-prep vendor); $220–280K band (removed posting); PD-generalizes loop (6 years stale); Accenture scale (own press release).

Every source describes what FDEs are *asked* to deliver, none whether it *works* — the load-bearing question for a career bet on the role.
