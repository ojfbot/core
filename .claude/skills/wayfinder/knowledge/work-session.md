Reference for `/wayfinder` work mode: resolution bookkeeping and map tending after a ticket is resolved.

- Post a resolution comment, close the issue, append a one-line gist + link to `## Decisions so far` (an **index, not a store** — the decision lives in its ticket/ADR).
- Tend the map: graduate fog whose question became statable; move ruled-out items to `## Out of scope`; flag tickets the new decision invalidated.
